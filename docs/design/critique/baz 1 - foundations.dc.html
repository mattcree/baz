baz — Foundations

What governs every screen. Visual mocks live on the critique board; references like (5a) point there.

THESIS

“A record archive after closing time — the works are lit, the room is not.” Your collection IS the interface: baz opens onto a wall of your own covers; click one, it plays. For people who own their music — collectors, Bandcamp buyers, rippers — arriving from foobar2000 and MusicBee.

The defensible position: prior art gives the collection 0–26% of the window at rest; baz gives it 73–100%. Nothing may trade this away. The failure mode to guard against is lobby — gorgeous at rest, useless in motion. The wall must be a working surface: filter, sort, and scale from 40 to 20,000 albums.

The shelf contains exactly two kinds of thing: artwork and type — plus one licensed exception: tiles for objects the user made (mixtapes, deferred) are set in type on a panel surface, never fake art.

THE FOUR ROOMS (8a, 8b)

A theme is a room, not a skin. One axis — room lightness, warm-neutral throughout. Ink and the accent arc oppose the room. Default is follow system: OS dark → Closing Time, OS light → Reading Room; Stone and Plaster are deliberate picks. Never park a room at oklch L .45–.58 — the dead zone where neither ink reaches contrast and mid-value sleeves melt.

ELEVATION (1a)

  • Four levels, ever: recess −1, wall 0, panel +1, float +2. A need for +3 means redesign.
  • Each step ≥ 0.03 oklch L from its neighbor. WCAG ratios are meaningless at these lightnesses — do not use them here.
  • Surfaces rise toward the lamp: lighter-and-warmer in dark rooms, darker in light rooms; recesses go the other way.
  • Hairline edges do the separating (ink at 8–14%, on the lit side); surface deltas do the grouping. No shadows, anywhere.
  • Recess −1 holds wells only: the groove, input-like regions.

THE ACCENT LAW

The accent states what is true about playback right now — the playing album's halo, the playing-track dot, the needle's fill — and nothing else. Never an opaque fill, never a button color, never decoration. Glow (soft bloom) exists only in Closing Time; elsewhere the halo is a 2px ring. State colors elsewhere: hover = ink at 6% overlay; selected = ink ring at 55%.

TYPE

  • IBM Plex Sans only; its digits are tabular by default, so columns rule themselves. No monospace, no second family.
  • Scale: 9–10px letter-spaced caps (0.14em) for shelf headers, group keys, and section labels — the only “chrome” voice; 11px for working UI text; 13px for names; poster sizes (40px+) belong to the Marquee lens and the filter query only.
  • Ink opacity is the hierarchy: 100% names, 65% working text, 40–45% metadata and labels, 35% disabled.

THE SKEUOMORPHISM RULE (9a, 9b)

The record supplies physics, structure, and vocabulary — the stack, the sides, groove spacing, “drop the needle” — never surface. Banned outright: vinyl discs peeking from sleeves, wood grain, tonearms, VU meters, wear/patina, any circle pretending to be a record. The audience owns the real objects; imitation reads as costume, quotation reads as respect.

THE REFUSALS LEDGER

Each of these was considered and rejected on principle. Re-opening one requires beating the argument, not forgetting it.

  • No autoplay, no radio. When the stack empties, baz goes silent. Silence is a feature.
  • No invisible shuffle pools. Shuffle draws only from what the wall shows (10b).
  • No auto-generated playlists — every crate and mixtape was made by a person.
  • No engagement stats. History records what happened; it never performs it back (no Wrapped, streaks, charts).
  • No user-picked accent color — the accent means playback truth; a picker dilutes it to decoration.
  • No view-options menus. Four group-key words and the lens switcher are the entire surface.
  • No captions at rest on the wall; captions state selection or playback only.
  • No mid-gray rooms (L .45–.58), no borders on artwork, no radii, no shadows, no motion (the toolkit has none — design for hard cuts).
