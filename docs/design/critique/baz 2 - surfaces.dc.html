baz — Surfaces & Workflows

Every surface: the decision, the spec, and its mock on the critique board. Scope tags: [v1] ships first; [defer] is designed but parked — see the Build guide.

The friction budget, binding on every feature: launch→music resumes <200ms (wall position and paused track restored) · intent→sound = 1 click · keystroke→filtered wall = next frame · new files→on the wall with no dialog, ever · tag fix inline, playback never blocked. Anything adding a click before sound argues for its life.

THE WALL — HOME [v1] (1b, board)

  • Covers ≥160px at default density; gutters ≈14% of cover width, vertical > horizontal. Square, borderless, unclipped.
  • No captions at rest. Selected: ink ring 55% + caption (title / artist·year). Playing: accent halo + caption + accent dot. Hover: ink 6% overlay.
  • Shelf breaks from the active group key: 9–10px caps headers at ink 40%, sticky in the virtualizer. Count top-right at ink 40%.
  • Group keys [v1]: ARTIST · YEAR · GENRE · ADDED · PLAYED — one row of words, no menus (10a, 12a). Genre verbatim from tags; messy tags show, honestly. CRATES joins later [defer].
  • Density is zoom, not a setting: 3–4 discrete stops; the full-collection overview (11b) is the last stop [defer].

FIND — TYPE ANYWHERE [v1] (4a)

  • No search field exists. Any bare keystroke filters the wall on the next frame; the query renders as display type (~48px) bottom-left, with match count.
  • Enter plays the first match; Esc clears. All other shortcuts live on a modifier layer (Cmd/Ctrl+…), since bare letters are query.
  • The index rail (11a) [v1]: a 36px type-only rail that is a projection of the active group key — ARTIST → A–Z · YEAR → decades · GENRE → genre names · ADDED/PLAYED → recency buckets (TODAY, THIS WEEK, months, NEVER). Change the key, the rail re-derives; it never has its own state. Click jumps, drag riffles, PgUp/PgDn snap a shelf. Fixed landmarks over infinite scroll — scroll works but is never the only way. Long value sets (many genres) elide to the shelves currently near the viewport plus first/last.

PLAYBACK — THE NEEDLE [v1] (5a, 9a)

  • No transport bar. A 2px seek line sits flush on the window's bottom edge, segmented by the album's real track lengths — 2px gaps at track boundaries, 6px at a side break. Fill is the accent; track is recess −1.
  • Wall label, bottom-left in 11px: “Title — Artist · elapsed”, plus stack status when queued (“· then 2 sleeves · 1h 58m left”). Bottom-right: “Queue · N”, opening the stack.
  • Click a groove segment to jump tracks — prev/next buttons don't exist. Keys: space play/pause, ←/→ seek, ↑/↓ or scroll volume, M mute.
  • Hovering the playing cover reveals transport glyphs over the art — the only icons in the app. Hard cut, no fade; must be usability-tested (no animation runtime).
  • The wall at rest is 100% collection; playback costs 2px and one line of type.

LENSES [v1: WALL + MARQUEE] (3a, 2a, 2b)

  • One library, shared state (selection, sort, filter, playing); the switcher is type — WALL · MARQUEE (· CRATE later) — keys 1/2/3.
  • Marquee (2a): the playing sleeve at half-window, full-bleed, poster type over a vertex-alpha scrim; the wall dims to 35% and waits. Default after ~30s idle while playing; any keystroke snaps back to the wall. Also hosts the pull (6b).
  • Crate (2b) [defer]: spine browsing; needs edge-sampled spine colors and rasterised rotated text — parked until proven cheap.
  • Lenses never grow settings. No grid-size pickers, no list mode, no column options.

INSPECTOR [v1] (9b, 12b)

  • 340px right panel on +1, hairline left edge; toggleable (Cmd+I). Art, title/artist/year, track list.
  • SIDE A / SIDE B headers when the rip's metadata carries sides — data-driven, never faked; playing track gets the accent dot.
  • The card: “PLAYED · N times since YYYY” with a column of date stamps (“12 Mar 2026 · side A only”). No charts.
  • Inline tag editing, field by field, playback never blocked. Bulk retagging is out of scope forever — Picard exists; punt gracefully. Format details (FLAC 24/96) live here, not in playback chrome.

THE STACK — THE QUEUE [v1] (6a, 13a)

  • Click always just plays. Shift+click stacks a sleeve (or a track, from the inspector) for later; a numeral chip on the cover is the only mark.
  • One queue holding whole sleeves and loose songs; popover on +2 from “Queue · N”; drag to reorder; albums listed as albums, never flattened.
  • Ephemeral by design: clears when it ends; the stack emptying means silence. “Pour into a mixtape” is the save path [defer with mixtapes]. No half-saved queue limbo.

SHUFFLE & THE PULL [v1] (10b, 6b)

  • One rule: shuffle plays from whatever the wall currently shows — a shelf, a filter's matches, or everything. The pool is always visible: non-pool covers dim to 35%; the next two draws carry faint ink rings.
  • “Vibe shuffle” is therefore a group key or a filter, not a feature. A future MOOD key (audio analysis) slots in without new UI.
  • The pull (⌘R): one sleeve, weighted toward the long-unplayed, presented in Marquee with “last played N years ago”. Nothing plays until space drops the needle; ⌘R pulls again, Esc returns.

HISTORY [v1] (12a, 12b)

  • An append-only ledger in a plain local file — one line per play; the user's to grep, back up, or burn. Last.fm scrobbling is an optional output, never a dependency.
  • Surfaces: the PLAYED group key (THIS EVENING → months → NEVER PLAYED), the inspector card's stamps, the pull's weighting. Nothing else.

FIRST RUN & IMPORT [v1] (4b)

  • Point baz at a folder once. Covers land on the wall as they're read; recess −1 squares mark what's still coming; playback works from the first cover. Header: “watching ~/Music · N of M imported”.
  • No importer dialog, no progress modal, ever. Ongoing watching uses the same surface: new rips appear under ADDED.

CURATION [defer] (13b, 13c)

  • Crates: album-level sets, hand-built, living as shelves under a CRATES group key; stored as plain text lists. Mixtapes: track-level, sequenced, SIDE A/B with optional C60/C90 budget (“1:48 left on side A”); serialized as .m3u8 beside the music; wall tile set in type on +1 — the licensed non-artwork tile.

SETTINGS

Not a screen. With zero-config import, no view options, and appearance as one control (follow system + four rooms), settings shrink to a small panel over the wall: music folder, appearance, scrobbling, output device. If it grows past one panel, something upstream went wrong.
