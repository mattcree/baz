# The surface-styling pass — rendered evidence

Pixel evidence for the pass that applies
[`.interface-design/system.md`](../../../../.interface-design/system.md) to
every surface baz has: [ADR-0017](../../../adr/0017-design-direction.md) steps
14 and 15, the icon-button treatment, and the three defects
[`04-fluidity.md`](../../04-fluidity.md) and
[`05-toolkit-and-visual-gap.md`](../../05-toolkit-and-visual-gap.md) measured.

Every image is the real binary. **Before** is `main` without this branch;
**after** is this branch. Same fixture, same window, same second of playback,
one variable.

## How these were made, and what was not touched

Captured per [`docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`](../../../DEVELOPMENT.md#headless-ui-verification):

- a private `Xvfb` per capture, `env -u WAYLAND_DISPLAY
  -u DBUS_SESSION_BUS_ADDRESS`, `WINIT_UNIX_BACKEND=x11`, no window manager, so
  the window is exactly the size it is asked for;
- **all six** redirections — scratch `HOME`, `XDG_DATA_HOME`,
  `XDG_CONFIG_HOME`, `XDG_CACHE_HOME`, `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, and no session-bus
  address — so the maintainer's library, config, thumbnails and session bus were
  never opened. Every run logs the receipt:

  ```
  [mpris] no session bus; desktop media controls unavailable (I/O error: path must be shorter than SUN_LEN)
  ```

- an 18-album / 120-track throwaway fixture: **digitally silent** FLAC (every
  sample a zero) with real Vorbis tags — artist, album, title, track, disc,
  date, genre, and ReplayGain figures on two records in five — so the surfaces
  under test have something honest to draw. Covers are generated by ImageMagick
  in twelve palettes and five compositions, **including near-black sleeves and
  five records with no cover at all**, because a wall of pleasant mid-tone art
  is not a wall this design has to survive. The build carries `device-output`
  so the transport is real; the scratch `HOME` carries an `.asoundrc` routing
  ALSA's default PCM to `null`, and `BAZ_DEVICE_TESTS` was never set;
- captures targeted at *this* process's window by pid, never "the active
  window". Every `Xvfb` and every `baz` started here was killed here; `ps` is
  clean.

## The measurements

Taken off the committed PNGs, not off the source.

### D1 — every alpha-expressed mark was composited in the wrong colour space

`theme.rs` named four marks as an alpha over the room's ink, with numbers from a
spec written in CSS. **CSS blends in sRGB; iced blends in linear light**
(`iced_graphics-0.13.0/src/color.rs:33`), so each one drew far louder than
specified. Now pre-composited to an opaque colour over the surface it lands on
(`Palette::ink_over`), which is immune to the blend space by construction:

| mark | before | reads as | after | reads as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the top bar's hairline | `#454442` | ink **26 %** | `#1B1C1C` | ink **7 %** — what the spec says |
| the search well's focus ring | `#A3A099` | ink **68 %** | `#6C6A67` | ink **45 %** — what the spec says |

Both match the predicted values to the byte.

### D2 — the accent was an opaque fill

`Play album`, over its 292 × 32 box, counting pixels that are *exactly*
`Palette::lamp` `#E3A14E`:

```
before   8 973 px of 9 344   (a slab)
after        50 px of 9 344   (a 1 px outline)
```

the product's standing rules: *amber is never an opaque fill — a ≤ 6 px mark, a 4 px rail,
a 1 px line, or light.*

### D6 — the wall's scrollbar was iced's, not baz's

Brightest byte anywhere in the scrollbar's 12 px lane down the wall
(x 1268–1279, y 60–760), summed over the lane:

```
before   39 062     (iced's stock light-grey bar, on a rail)
after     7 141     (baz's scroller, no rail)
```

### The mute glyph and the fader

Centre of mass of the mute glyph against the centre of the fader's rail, both
measured by luminance profile in the real render:

```
before   glyph y = 809.2   rail y = 816.3   offset  −7.0 px
after    glyph y = 815.5   rail y = 815.2   offset  +0.3 px
```

The residual is the speaker glyph's own asymmetry, not placement.

## What to look at

**The wall.** Before: a card raises behind the selected sleeve, a band of
`plinth` sits behind its caption, a ring wraps art *and* type, and every sleeve
carries a drop shadow. After: artwork, type, and a rule under the label — 1 px
hovered, 2 px selected. Nothing is drawn on or around a work.

**The bar.** Before: five little bordered boxes strung along a surface whose
whole thesis is that chrome recedes, and the rest and disabled states of each
one are the same picture. After: the glyphs, at 0.57 ink, in the same 32 px
targets.

**The placeholder sleeves.** Before, the records baz knows *least* about were
the brightest objects on the wall. After, they are its quietest.

**The inspector.** Four lines of metadata became a header, a track list with
disc headers and a ruled duration column, and a **Details** block of up to
twelve fields — every one of them a thing the scan actually read.
